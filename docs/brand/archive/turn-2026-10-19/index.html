Three states on a path that turns a corner. The corner state is the current one, lit in the UI blue. No outlines, three-face shading, graphite elsewhere.

Bridgeschosen

Pitch 8.5 instead of 7, bridges 1.6 wide and level with the block tops: both transitions show, so it reads as a path.

Trace

No bridges, a thin graphite trace on the ground: from this angle the blocks hide most of it.

Close

No bridges, a one-unit gap, the 16 px drawing at full size: true to the favicon, but it is the pile again.

Round three

Pitch 7: the bridge into the front block hides behind it.

Bridges is round three's drawing with its two faults fixed. The space between states is now wider than a block is deep, so the three blocks stop packing into one lump. Both bridges sit level with the block tops, where nothing in front can hide them. Trace loses its line behind the blocks, and Close is the pile again. The 32 px drawing keeps both bridges. The 16 px drawing drops them and keeps the three states.
